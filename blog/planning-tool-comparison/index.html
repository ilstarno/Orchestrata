Buying a scheduling product and choosing a solver are different decisions. Start with the workflow you need: who owns the schedule, what happens when it changes, and who approves the result.

Test one change to a real plan

Pick a completed shift or dispatch window, remove one qualified person, and rerun it with the same deadlines and constraints. Check which commitments stay fixed, which assignments move, and who reviews the exceptions. Compare solve time only when the inputs, constraints, objective, and time budget match. This guide does not rank speed or solution quality.

In the Orchestrata payment replay, the same 24 cases are compared before and after a reviewer outage. Scheduled work drops from 15 to 13; held work rises from 8 to 10. The first affected case needs 30 minutes of independent review, but the backup has only one 10-minute slot before the cutoff. That is a result for this example, not a production benchmark.
