Start with your workflow and the system it lives in

Orchestrata’s catalog documents examples across payment reviews, claims work, sales lead assignment, contact-center staffing, field service, deliveries, and airport operations. Browse the worked solutions to see the inputs, the rules that bind the plan, and the decisions still left for a person.

On the integrations page, Salesforce, Jira, ServiceNow, and Workday examples show where a planning decision fits in those workflows. Public replays do not demonstrate live connections or write-back. In a pilot, start with an approved extract and confirm any connection or data movement separately.

Rerun the same disruption

Take one historical shift or dispatch window. Remove one qualified person, keep the original deadlines and rules, and compare what stays fixed, what moves, and what needs review. Compare solve time only with matched inputs, constraints, goals, and budgets. This guide does not rank speed or solution quality.

In the payment review example, scheduled cases drop from 15 to 13 after a reviewer outage. One newly held case needs 30 minutes of independent review; the backup has one 10-minute slot before the cutoff.
