Planning by platform

See the records to bring, the proposed assignment, and who reviews it.

These examples don’t connect to or update vendor systems. They don’t indicate a vendor partnership.

Workday Cover an open shift after a callout
Data to bring
Bring an approved list of shifts, roles, and available staff. Leave out employee records that are not needed.
Proposed assignment
Show the replacement and any other shift that would change.
Review or hold
A manager checks coverage and work rules; a shift with no eligible person remains open.

Workday: Scheduling settings and schedule score

No Workday connection, payroll change, or formal leave decision.

See the worked exampleOfficial reference (Workday)Workday REST
Jira Service Management Choose who can take the next request
Data to bring
Bring queue items, their urgency, and the team’s skills and availability.
Proposed assignment
Suggest an available owner for each eligible request; leave the rest visible.
Review or hold
The queue lead checks the urgent items and exceptions before changing any assignee.

Atlassian: Jira Service Management · Pop out queues

No Jira connection, reassignment, or ticket transition.

See the worked exampleOfficial reference (Jira Service Management)Jira Cloud REST
Skedulo Reassign a field job when someone is unavailable
Data to bring
Bring job windows, required skills, and approved worker availability.
Proposed assignment
Compare a replacement against the jobs already assigned in the same window.
Review or hold
A dispatcher reviews the change. If no one is eligible, the job stays unassigned.

Skedulo: Scheduling Console

No Skedulo connection, route calculation, dispatch, or mobile notification.

See the worked exampleOfficial reference (Skedulo)Skedulo GraphQL
Epic · Healthcare Match approved operating-room blocks to rooms
Data to bring
Bring anonymized time blocks and the room options cleared by your operations team.
Proposed assignment
Place overlapping blocks in different eligible rooms.
Review or hold
A facility scheduler checks the proposed room allocation and holds blocks without an approved match.

Mount Sinai training: Appointment Desk in Epic

Use approved scheduling and room references only. No booking, patient information, or medical decisions.

See the worked exampleOfficial reference (Epic · Healthcare)Epic SMART on FHIR
SITA Operations Manager · Airports Find gates for overlapping aircraft
Data to bring
Bring an airport-approved list of gates eligible for each aircraft and its turnaround window.
Proposed assignment
Assign separate compatible gates to simultaneous flights.
Review or hold
Airport operations checks the proposed gates and holds any conflict without a compatible gate.

SITA Mission Control (different SITA product)

Ask for an authorized flight export and a separate list of compatible gates. No live gate changes or safety clearance.

See the worked exampleOfficial reference (SITA Operations Manager · Airports)IATA AIDX
NetLine/Ops ++ · Airlines Cover simultaneous flight duties
Data to bring
Bring flight duties and a list of crews the airline has already cleared for each duty.
Proposed assignment
Assign different eligible crews to duties that overlap.
Review or hold
Crew control checks the proposed coverage and holds duties that still need a crew.

Lufthansa Systems: NetLine/Ops ++ aiOCC

Use an approved flight-duty export and a separate list of cleared crews. Crew control remains responsible for duty and rest checks.
