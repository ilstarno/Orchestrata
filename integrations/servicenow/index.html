Planning workflows

  • Incident assignment

    Match incoming incidents to teams with the right skills, time before the service deadline, and room for more work. A supervisor reviews incidents without a match.

  • Change windows

    Find a window outside blackout periods, without overlapping changes to the same service. The change manager reviews conflicts before approving the schedule.

  • Major incidents and on-call coverage

    Check who is on call, who can take over, and which skills are available for a major incident. The incident lead confirms coverage.

  • Service catalog requests

    Assign requests to teams that handle that catalog item. Check prerequisites, service deadlines, and workload; send blocked work to the request owner.

  • Field service jobs

    Match jobs to available workers by skills, parts, travel time, and customer windows. The dispatcher reviews jobs that do not fit.

  • Approval routing

    Identify who is authorized to approve a request and whether they’re available before the deadline. The designated approver still makes the decision.
