What this connector does

Use queue demand to inform a staffing discussion and prepare a blank schedule draft.

Data in scope

Read queue conversation aggregates for the approved scope. Aggregates describe observed demand; they are not a complete employee schedule or availability feed.

Reads and changes

Read queue conversation aggregates and prepare a separately authorized request for a blank schedule draft. Review and publishing belong to the organization’s scheduling process.

Access to arrange

Agree the Genesys region, organization and queue scope. Keep aggregate-read permissions separate from any permission to create a schedule draft.

What to check before relying on it

A blank draft is a starting point for the scheduling team. Supply the reviewed shift content through your established Genesys scheduling process.

Plan a scoped pilot

Start with the records and decision described above. Agree the permitted data, account access and review owner before any connection. Use the listed records, permissions and decision to define the scope.
