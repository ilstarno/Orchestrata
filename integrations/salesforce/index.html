Planning with Salesforce

See how teams could use their Salesforce records to make day-to-day planning decisions.

The worked examples run separately. These workflows show what to check and who should review the decision; they do not read or write Salesforce records.

Salesforce Trailhead: Tour the Service Console

Use the Salesforce edition you already have

Start with records the company has approved for this workflow. No edition needs write access for a first comparison.

  • Free Suite and Starter Suite: start with an approved file or prepared example. Don’t assume report export or API access.
  • Pro Suite: use an authorized report export if available, or check whether your organization has the Web Services API add-on.
  • Core, Advanced, and Max: check API eligibility, the connected app’s access, and what records and fields it can read. Legacy contracts may say Enterprise, Unlimited, or Agentforce 1.

Check Service Cloud, Omni-Channel, Field Service, Revenue Cloud, and approval tools separately. Availability depends on the contract, add-ons, permissions, and setup.

Salesforce workflow scenarios

Sales

  • Lead assignment

    Select an available rep by territory, language, and open workload. Sales operations reviews leads without a match.

  • Lead enrichment and conversion

    Check required fields, exact email matches and account ownership. Review conflicting matches before creating or reusing a contact.

  • Territory coverage

    Check account location, rep coverage and workload before changing territory assignments. Sales leaders review gaps.

  • Opportunity coverage

    Choose which active opportunities need support using pursuit stage, skills and available time. Sales managers review competing requests.

  • Renewal coverage

    Assign work before renewal dates using contract deadlines, account knowledge and team availability. Account owners review gaps.

Service

  • Omni-Channel queues

    Review how skills, queue priority, agent availability and workload affect who can take the next case.

  • Escalated or reopened cases

    Compare case priority, service deadlines and past ownership; let a supervisor review conflicts before reassigning.

  • Service staffing

    Check voice and chat coverage by time window, skills and breaks. Supervisors decide how to cover gaps.

Field service

  • Work orders

    Check technician skills, availability, parts and customer windows before suggesting an assignment for dispatcher review.

  • Emergency replanning

    Keep work already underway fixed. Compare available crews, customer commitments and travel time before a dispatcher moves other jobs.

  • Route and load capacity

    Check pickup-before-delivery order, vehicle limits and receiving windows. The dispatcher reviews work that does not fit.

  • Asset maintenance

    Compare maintenance windows, compatible crew and parts availability. The asset owner confirms which work can be scheduled.

Approvals and handoffs

  • Quote exceptions

    Find required approvers for discounts or terms and check their availability. An authorized reviewer makes the decision.

  • Order handoffs and backlog

    Compare order readiness, handoff deadlines and fulfillment capacity. Operations reviews orders that remain blocked.
