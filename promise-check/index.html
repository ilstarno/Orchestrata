What the numbers mean

Model cost = fixed round-trip travel minutes + twice the overtime minutes + penalties for held jobs. Lift repair: 260; inspection: 230; maintenance: 200; urgent isolation: 1,000. These weights are sample priorities, not currency, measured savings or an assurance that every job is covered.

Skills, availability, latest starts, the confirmed inspection and the selected overtime cap are required. Each technician takes at most one job. An unassigned job remains a decision for the dispatcher.

A small dispatch window, not a weekly route plan

Travel estimates and durations are fixed sample inputs. No live traffic, multi-stop routing, weekly calendar, new hire or contractor sourcing is modeled. This page accepts no real-data uploads and does not write to your systems.
