Missing a column?

Put the column names in the first row. If a title comes first, choose the heading row if it appears in the first four rows. If the headings start farther down, move them up or export just the table. Use values, not formulas.

Job name and required skill describe what needs doing. Person name and skills identify who can do it. Available and Job limit keep the plan from assigning work to someone who is out or already at capacity. Add any missing column to the workbook and reopen it.
