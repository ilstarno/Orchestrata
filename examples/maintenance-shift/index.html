Worked example · Sample data · Local Workbench

Assign a maintenance shift and keep unassigned work visible

Twelve work orders, six technicians and explicit eligibility rules. The local Workbench returns ten assignments and leaves two orders unassigned.

1. Bring the three input sheets together

This example runs in the separate Orchestrata Workbench application on a local device. It uses a workbook and the bundled maintenance solver; no provider connection or hosted session is involved.

Download the sample workbook. Its three sheets describe the orders, each technician’s capacity and availability, and the permitted order–technician pairs with cost and delay values.

  • Work orders: twelve identifiers, including pump-a, hv-isolator and lift-controller.
  • Technicians: six identifiers. For example, mechanical-east has three slots; hv-standby is unavailable.
  • Eligibility: twenty permitted pairs. An absent pair does not authorize an assignment.

2. Correct the flagged availability value

In the maintenance workflow, open the workbook and review the column mapping. The sample deliberately contains confirm in the first technician’s availability cell. The Workbench asks for a true/false value. For this sample, enter true, save the correction, and confirm the reviewed input before running.

3. Run the cost plan and review what remains

Select the cost objective and choose Run locally. This run assigns ten orders with a total of 14,600 sample cost cents. Every assignment uses an eligible, available technician within the supplied capacity.

4. Compare the objective and export the result

Changing to the delay objective and running again also assigns ten orders. It places pump-a and pump-b with mechanical-east, and cooling-loop and compressor with mechanical-west. The other assignments and the two unassigned orders stay the same.

The returned delay total is 292 sample minutes: the sum of the selected pair values, not elapsed shift duration. Cost and delay are different objectives and units; these totals are not a savings comparison.
