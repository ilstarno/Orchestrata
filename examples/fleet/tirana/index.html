      Rules used

      What this case checks

      Eight fixed street routes and 32 mapped stops. This case does not reroute or optimize all routes together.

      • R01 · Company policy · Both handoffs must be on the fixed route, with pickup before delivery.
      • R02 · Company policy · The route must use an allowed mode for the shipment.
      • R03 · Company policy · The courier must be available and their assigned equipment in service.
      • R04 · Company policy · At pickup, the shipment and current load must fit the available payload.
      • R05 · Company policy · Wait if early. Both handoffs must finish within their own time windows.
      • R06 · Company policy · The route must finish before the courier’s shift ends.
      • R07 · Company policy · Allow one request per fixed route. Rank feasible requests by priority, then by request ID.
      • R09 · Company policy · The courier needs the right handling qualification. The parcel must fit inside the vehicle or bag.
      • R10 · Company policy · Borrowing another team’s courier needs approval in this case.
      • R11 · Company policy · Missing road or curb evidence puts the request on hold. Scenario evidence is not a permit.
      • R08 · Checks and evidence · The route follows the prepared street profile. Segment times are estimates, not live traffic.

      Sources and limits

      Company rules are assumptions for this case. The map provides a routing model, not an assessment of current restrictions or legal compliance.

      • Tirana Municipality: check whether city ring access requires authorization.
      • Tirana Parking: check signs and the handoff location. A parking tariff does not authorize loading.
      • Tirana Parking: check the sign, hours, and allowed activity for the reserved bay.
      • Official Publications Centre: Road Code register. The case does not determine which provisions apply.

      This case uses prepared street routes and a separate calculation. It does not run live. Reproduce the case.
