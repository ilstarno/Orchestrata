Pipeline stages

How the pipeline works

  1. 01

    Intake

    Capture the company, work email, territory, and language from the inbound lead.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Check the required fields and trim spaces from the email address. Use lowercase for this example’s email match.

  3. 03

    Match

    Look for an exact email match. Link only a unique match from the same account; send conflicting matches for review.

  4. 04

    Assign

    Prefer the existing contact owner for lead assignment if they are available and eligible. Otherwise, select the available rep with the lightest workload who covers the lead’s territory and language.

  5. 05

    Qualify

    Confirm the lead meets the qualification criteria. If not, keep it with its assigned rep for follow-up.

  6. 06

    Convert

    For a qualified lead, create a contact or link the matching record without duplicating it.
