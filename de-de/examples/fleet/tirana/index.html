      Angewandte Regeln

      Was dieses Beispiel prüft

      Eight fixed street geometries, 32 mapped example stops; no rerouting, live dispatch or global route optimization.

      • R01 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · Mapped pickup and delivery must both match; sequence 2 precedes 3.
      • R02 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · The route mode must be among the modes allowed for the shipment.
      • R03 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · The courier must be available and their assigned asset in service.
      • R04 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · At pickup, shipment plus current load cannot exceed payload capacity.
      • R05 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · Wait if early; both pickup and delivery service must finish by each window's end.
      • R06 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · The planned route must finish by the courier's shift end.
      • R07 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · Only one request per fixed route; choose feasible requests by priority, then stable request ID.
      • R09 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · Operator must be qualified for the requested handling; cargo must fit the configured internal dimensions.
      • R10 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · Borrowing another team’s operator requires simulated cross-team approval.
      • R11 · Unternehmensrichtlinie · Missing scenario road or curb evidence holds the request for review. Sample evidence is not a permit.
      • R08 · Prüfungen und Nachweise · Travel follows the already mapped OSM profile; segment durations are estimates, not live traffic.

      Quellen und Grenzen

      Company rules are scenario assumptions. OSM profile/access is a routing model only; legal compliance and current road restrictions were not evaluated.

      • Municipality of Tirana: ring circulation authorization. Verify whether the route, vehicle and time require authorization inside the city ring.
      • Tirana Parking: on-street paid parking. Parking tariffs do not confirm lawful loading; check actual signs and allowed handoff location.
      • Tirana Parking: reserved spaces. Check any sign, activity, applicable hours and permission for the specific bay.
      • Official Publications Centre: Road Code register. Registry reference only; the case does not determine which provisions apply.

      The case uses prepared street routes and a standalone calculation. It is not a live routing run. Reproduce the case.
