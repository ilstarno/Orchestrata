Match pickup and delivery jobs to vehicles

Explore matching three overlapping delivery jobs to eligible vans by per-job capacity and availability, without claiming live vehicle routing.

Three jobs with overlapping service windows

A parcel pickup, depot delivery, and small-box drop have different loads and service windows. Each van has a weight capacity and an availability window. The example filters eligible choices by those values before solving assignment.

Model the rules

  • A van must have capacity for the individual job’s load.
  • Availability must cover that job’s full service window.
  • Overlapping jobs use distinct vans; the late-start van is preferred for the depot delivery.

Check the example result

JobVehicle
Parcel pickup · 450 kgShort-shift van
Depot delivery · 700 kgLate-start van
Small-box drop · 150 kgLarge van

The short-shift van can carry the parcel and cover its window. The late-start van covers the depot delivery and the preference. The large van handles the remaining drop. The synthetic fixture returns [1, 2, 0], with no hard or soft penalties.

What this example does not do

This is eligibility and assignment, not vehicle routing. It does not order stops, estimate travel, combine loads across legs, or enforce pickup-before-delivery sequence.

Reproduce it locally

Use the checked-in synthetic fixture from the recipe catalog under review. From that repository’s root:

(cd backend && cargo run -p orchestrata-cli -- ../recipes/examples/fleet-pickup-delivery/problem.json)
