Match pickup and delivery jobs to vehicles

Explore matching three overlapping delivery jobs to eligible vans by per-job capacity and availability, without claiming live vehicle routing.

Outcome on the sample

All three overlapping deliveries fit the vehicles assigned to them. The heavier depot delivery goes to the van that meets its weight and availability requirements.

Match three overlapping jobs to vans

The depot delivery carries 700 kg and runs from 09:10 to 09:50. It needs a van that can handle that weight and remain available for the entire job. The parcel pickup and small-box drop overlap it, so they cannot use the same van.

What to provide

Recipe fieldTypeWhat to enter
job-namesList of stringsJob labels in order
job-weights-kgList of integersWhole kilograms per job
job-windows-minutesList of integersStart and end pairs in minutes after midnight
vehicle-namesList of stringsVan labels in index order
vehicle-capacities-kgList of integersUsable load limit in kilograms per van
vehicle-windows-minutesList of integersAvailability start and end pairs on the same day
eligible-pairsList of integersJob and vehicle index pairs after checking weight and availability
preferred-depot-vehicleOptional integer2, the late-start van
VanCapacityAvailability
Large van1,000 kg08:30–10:20
Short-shift van500 kg08:50–09:40
Late-start van800 kg09:05–10:30

Use the source inputs to inspect the original minute values. Before solving, the example turns each job’s eligible vehicles into an integer domain. The solver receives those domains, a hard all_different rule, and one soft prefer_value rule with weight 1. It does not compute compatibility from weights or times during the solve.

What comes back

JobLoadService windowAssigned
Parcel pickup450 kg09:00–09:30Short-shift van
Depot delivery700 kg09:10–09:50Late-start van
Small-box drop150 kg09:15–10:00Large van

The late-start van cannot take the parcel pickup because it is not available at 09:00. The short-shift van cannot take the depot delivery because of both load and availability. The expected result is values: [1, 2, 0] in job order, hard and soft scores 0/0, and 8 combinations checked.

Run it

Save the normalized problem and run:

orchestrata-cli problem.json

Compare the expected output, or run the problem on the local API.

Where this example stops

These are independent jobs in overlapping windows. The example does not order stops or calculate travel time, group loads across stops, link a pickup to a delivery, or produce a route. The catalog’s larger benchmark inputs generate stop data, not solved routes or measured route performance.
