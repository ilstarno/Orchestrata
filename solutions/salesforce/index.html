Salesforce planning workspace

A workspace inside Salesforce for assigning jobs, reviewing exceptions, and approving changes without switching tools.

From work orders to approved assignments

  1. Plan the jobs. Start from the work that needs assigning and the people or resources that can take it.
  2. Review the plan. See proposed assignments and exceptions that need a decision before anything changes.
  3. Approve updates. Give the person responsible a chance to review and approve changes without switching tools.

Workflow preview

This is an interface concept, not a live Salesforce integration. It does not represent a Salesforce partnership or certification.
