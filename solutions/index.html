    See claims service dispatch
    Outcome and measures

    What to measure: Appointments kept and work waiting for a coordinator.

  • Sales operations

    Who should follow up with an inbound lead?

    See lead routing and contact conversion
    Outcome and measures

    Follow 60 sample leads. The rules compare territory, language, availability, and current workload before suggesting an owner. The preview distinguishes a new contact, a link to an existing contact, and a lead waiting for qualification or manual review. It does not update a CRM.

    What to measure: Time to first follow-up, leads without an owner, and conflicting contact matches.

  • Workforce

    Who covers the shift after a callout?

    Compare the staffing plans
    Outcome and measures

    All four shifts stay covered in the example. Two assignments change, and the planner can see which preferences no longer fit.

    What to measure: Unfilled shifts and last-minute reassignments.

  • Fleet and deliveries

    Which vehicle can take each delivery?

    See the delivery assignments
    Outcome and measures

    The New York example assigns 15,876 of 16,030 stops to 100 couriers. The remaining 154 are on hold.

    What to measure: Unassigned deliveries and missed delivery windows.

  • Warehouse operations

    Which dock takes each trailer?

    See the dock assignments
    Outcome and measures

    Three overlapping arrivals get distinct eligible doors. Parcel outbound keeps the door close to its staging area.

    What to measure: Trailer wait time and conflicting dock bookings.

  • Cargo bookings

    Which sailing can take each shipment?

    See the cargo allocation
    Outcome and measures

    Six consignments fit across three sample sailings without exceeding their separate booking weight allowances. Refrigerated cargo goes to eligible sailings.

    This example is a booking allocation, not a stowage plan or a booking confirmation.

    What to measure: Deferred consignments and unused booking allowance.

  • Field service

    Which technician takes each job?

    See the technician assignments
    Outcome and measures

    Three simultaneous jobs get eligible technicians without double-booking anyone. The preferred technician still takes the lift repair.

    What to measure: Unassigned jobs and repeat dispatch changes.

  • Contact center

    Who covers each support queue?

    See the queue coverage
    Outcome and measures

    Eligible operators cover billing escalations, Spanish-language support, and account recovery without assigning anyone to two queues at once.

    This staffing example does not route individual calls or connect to a phone system.

    What to measure: Uncovered queue time and staffing reassignments.

  • Airport operations

    Which gates can take overlapping flights?

    See the gate assignments
    Outcome and measures

    Three flights get compatible gates at the same time, and Flight 201 keeps its preferred gate.

    What to measure: Gate conflicts and late gate changes.
