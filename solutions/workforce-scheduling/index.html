Reassign shifts after a staffing change

Compare shift assignments across two retail stores before and after a callout, while keeping required coverage and checking missed preferences.

One callout across two stores

The North and South stores each need two workers for overlapping shifts. Ben becomes unavailable. Keep the four shifts, hard rules, and preference weights the same, and compare the baseline with a run that removes Ben from the eligible choices.

Model the rules

  • Only qualified, available workers appear in the eligible choices.
  • Four simultaneous positions need distinct people.
  • Keep Ana fixed as North keyholder, and prefer familiar assignments where possible.

Check the example result

Clara covers the North floor. Elena takes the South register so Clara is not double-booked. Both plans cover all four positions. The adjusted score is −8 because it misses two preferences, not because it violates a mandatory rule.

What this example does not do

This compares two fixed-shift assignments. It does not forecast demand, verify labor rules, track rest periods, or build a weekly staffing roster.

Reproduce it locally

Use the checked-in synthetic fixture from the recipe catalog under review. From that repository’s root:
