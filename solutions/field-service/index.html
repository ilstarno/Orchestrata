Assign technicians to overlapping jobs

See how prequalified technicians can be assigned to three simultaneous service jobs without double-booking. Inspect the checked-in synthetic example.

Three service appointments at the same time

The same time slot contains a lift repair, a safety inspection, and preventive maintenance. Three technicians have different qualifications. List eligible people for each visit before asking the solver to assign the jobs.

Model the rules

  • A technician takes at most one appointment in the time slot.
  • Each job can use only a technician from its eligible list.
  • Prefer Alex for the lift repair if every appointment can still be covered.

Check the example result

AppointmentTechnician
Lift repairAlex
Safety inspectionSam
Preventive maintenanceNoor

Alex is eligible for the repair and inspection, but assigning Sam to the inspection leaves Alex available for the preferred repair. Noor takes preventive maintenance. The synthetic fixture returns [0, 1, 2], with no hard or soft penalties.

What this example does not do

This is a fixed-slot assignment example. It does not choose appointment times, calculate travel, run dispatch, or plan a technician’s full day.

Reproduce it locally

Use the checked-in synthetic fixture from the recipe catalog under review. From that repository’s root:

(cd backend && cargo run -p orchestrata-cli -- ../recipes/examples/field-service/problem.json)
