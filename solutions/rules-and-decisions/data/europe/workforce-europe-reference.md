# Workforce scheduling: Europe and the UK

**Official sources checked on 16 November 2026.** This reference covers selected ordinary adult employment rules, not every obligation. It is not legal advice, a payroll or leave-entitlement calculator, or a claim that a schedule complies. The [JSON constraints catalog](workforce-europe-constraints.json) is a review reference. It does not switch any rule on.

## Differences that can change a schedule

| Place | General hours limits or baseline | Rest, breaks and paid annual leave in the ordinary case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England, Wales, Scotland | Generally 48 hours per seven days on average, including overtime, usually assessed over 17 weeks. Do not infer an opt-out. | 11 consecutive hours daily; ordinarily 24 hours weekly, with a statutory 14-day option; a 20-minute uninterrupted break for more than six hours under the default terms. Usually 5.6 weeks paid annual leave, up to 28 statutory days for regular hours. [Working Time Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1998/1833/2026-10-01?view=extent&wrap=true) · [Annual leave](https://www.gov.uk/holiday-entitlement-rights) |
| Northern Ireland | Has separate regulations; ordinarily 48 average hours including overtime, with a 17-week default reference period. | Similar headline values for ordinary adult workers: daily rest 11 continuous hours, weekly default 24 hours, and default 20-minute break when working more than six hours. Its own leave framework combines four weeks plus 1.6 weeks, with the statutory 28-day total cap. [NI regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/nisr/2016/49/body) · [NI rest guidance](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/rest-breaks) · [NI leave](https://www.nidirect.gov.uk/articles/holiday-entitlements) |
| France, metropolitan private-sector example | A normal full-time week is 35 hours, not the cap. General ceilings: 10 hours per day, 48 in one week, and 44 on average over 12 consecutive weeks. | Ordinary daily rest is 11 hours and weekly rest totals 35 hours. A 20-minute consecutive break is due by six consecutive working hours. The normal accrual is 2.5 *jours ouvrables* per effective month, commonly 30 *jours ouvrables* over a full effective year. [Work hours](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1911) · [Daily rest](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F990) · [Weekly rest](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2327) · [Paid leave](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2258) |
| Germany | Ordinary limit of eight hours per statutory working day. Up to ten requires compensation to the eight-hour average within six calendar months or 24 weeks. | Daily rest normally 11 hours. Breaks: 30 minutes if more than six and no more than nine hours; 45 if more than nine. Break segments are at least 15 minutes, with no more than six continuous hours of work. Annual leave minimum 24 *Werktage* on the statutory six-day basis, not 24 scheduled days for every worker. [Working Time Act §§3–5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/BJNR117100994.html) · [Annual Leave Act §3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/burlg/__3.html) |
| Italy | Normal schedule is 40 hours weekly, subject to applicable terms. The 48-hour weekly average includes overtime and generally uses at most four months without an authorized longer reference period. | Daily rest is ordinarily 11 continuous hours in 24. Weekly rest generally includes a 24-hour stretch combined with daily rest, averaged over no more than 14 days. For more than six hours, check collective break terms and the applicable ten-minute minimum. Annual paid leave minimum is four weeks. [Ministry working-time guidance](https://www.lavoro.gov.it/en/single-digital-gateway/terms-and-conditions-employment/working-hours) · [Leave under D.Lgs. 66/2003](https://distaccoue.lavoro.gov.it/it-it/Aree-Tematiche/Area-Tematica/id/1) |
| Netherlands | For ordinary adult employees, general limits are 12 hours per shift, 60 per week, 55 averaged over four weeks and 48 averaged over 16 weeks. Review any permitted exception separately. | Daily rest ordinarily 11 hours; weekly rest ordinarily 36 consecutive hours per seven 24-hour periods. The law has a separate 72-hour/14-day option. Standard breaks are 30 minutes when work exceeds 5.5 hours and 45 when it exceeds ten, with split segments of at least 15 minutes. Annual leave minimum is four times contracted weekly hours. [Working Hours Act guidance](https://business.gov.nl/staff/terms-of-employment/working-hours-act/) · [Weekly rest alternatives](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/themas/werk/werktijden/vraag-en-antwoord/werktijden/wettelijke-regels-werktijden-en-rusttijden) · [Paid leave](https://business.gov.nl/staff/terms-of-employment/holiday-entitlement-for-employees/) |
| Poland | Basic system: eight hours per day, average 40 per week in an average five-day week over the applicable period. The general weekly average including overtime is capped at 48. | Daily rest generally 11 hours and weekly 35 including daily rest. In-work break is at least 15 minutes at six hours, another 15 above nine, another 15 above 16. Full-time annual leave baseline is 20 days below ten years of qualifying service and 26 at ten or more. Use HR-confirmed qualifying service, not a start-date shortcut. [Labour Code](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2026/1245/ogl) · [Breaks](https://www.gov.pl/web/family/breaks) · [Leave](https://www.gov.pl/web/family/the-duration-of-annual-leave) |

The [EU working-time directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32003L0088) is useful context for EU member states, not a common switch for these schedules. A sector, regulated role, worker category, multiple jobs, night work, or lawful derogation can change the calculation. A contract, collective agreement, and company policy must be assessed for their own scope and authority. A local agreement must never be presumed, nor should a constraint be relaxed just because it makes a roster infeasible.

## Holidays, approved leave and site closures are different inputs

- **Public holidays** are versioned by the *place of work* and local date. [Official UK calendar](https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays) separates England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland. [French rules](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2405) distinguish May 1, authorized continuity of service, and territorial differences. German statutory holiday rules use the relevant state and local calendar, with [replacement rest](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbzg/__11.html) for permitted work. Italy has [national and local observances](https://presidenza.governo.it/ufficio_cerimoniale/cerimoniale/giornate.html); [Law 151/2025](https://www.gazzettaufficiale.it/eli/id/2025/10/10/25G00153/SG) adds the national holiday on October 4, effective 1 January 2026. In the [Netherlands](https://business.gov.nl/staff/terms-of-employment/public-holidays-in-the-netherlands/) a public holiday does not itself provide automatic time off. In Poland, a non-Sunday public holiday generally [reduces reference-period working hours by eight](https://www.gov.pl/web/family/standards-and-quantities-of-working-time2); do not turn that calculation into a blanket closure.
- **Opening hours, extra pay, notice periods, swap rules and staffing floors** need their own authority and scope. A company closure must not be represented as a statutory ban; a union-negotiated restriction must not be represented as an optional preference.
- **Leave and other absences:** HR records entitlement and approvals. Pass the planner only the dated availability effect and any HR-approved scheduling rule classification. Do not import diagnoses, pregnancy status, leave justifications, family details, or full employee profiles. Keep any identifying lookup separately protected. Do not use a staffing surrogate to determine leave eligibility, assess a worker, or make any employment decision.

## Keep the reason and the date

Apply the correct profile for the *shift date*: place of work, sector, role, working-time system, relevant agreement, and local time basis. Preserve local calendar dates for holidays and reference periods; use elapsed time for continuous-rest checks where applicable. Store start/end instants and local zone rather than assuming a calendar day is always 24 elapsed hours. Confirm how the law accounts for work, breaks, on-call time and reference-period exclusions. Incomplete history or unresolved applicability must produce **review required**, not a passed or failed compliance result.

For each check, save the exact rule ID/version, source and provision, effective date evidence, applicable local reference window, measured quantity and units, threshold, result, and who approved any authorized exception. Mark which constraints came from law, a collective agreement, a contract, or employer policy. Limit audit access and avoid personal explanations.

**Illustrative audit record:** work in a Paris example ends at 22:00 local on 16 November and restarts at 08:00 on 17 November. The interval is 600 elapsed minutes. Against a verified ordinary-rule profile, this is below the 660-minute daily-rest minimum; hold the proposed assignment for review. If there is no validated role or agreement classification, the reason is **missing applicability evidence**, not a conclusion about compliance.
