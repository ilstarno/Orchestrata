Transport, fleet, and cargo

Where the cargo goes

The cutaways show allocation by ship. Hold positions and box sizes do not represent a stowage plan.

What the calculation checks

  1. Eligibility first. Ship choices are supplied for each consignment. Chilled produce and frozen berries can only use Boreal or Meridian. Other eligibility lists are sample inputs too.
  2. Allowances are per ship. The capacity rule adds all assigned tonnes on each ship and compares the total with its own allowance.
  3. Preferences come after required rules. Among eligible allocations within the limits, the result also matches all six sample ship preferences.

What this example does not check

This is a sample assignment using stated whole-ship weight allowances, not guidance for loading a vessel. It does not check stability, slot dimensions, weight distribution, securing, hazardous-goods segregation, reefer power, or maritime requirements. The site shows a recorded engine result and does not run a solver in the browser.
