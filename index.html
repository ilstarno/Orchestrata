The Kubernetes analogy

Kubernetes places software workloads. Orchestrata places work against limits on people, equipment and time.

Review an interview schedule

See why two items went to review
What changed
When a specialist became unavailable, one interview kept its time with a qualified substitute. Another interview and the debrief that depended on it went to review.
Why
The second interview had only one offered time and no qualified specialist available at that time.
What to check
A coordinator checks for another qualified interviewer or offers a new time. The confirmed meetings stay in place.
