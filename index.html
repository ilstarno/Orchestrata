Match work to the resources you have

What you give it and what you get back

  1. List the work and eligible choices. Say which people, vehicles, or equipment can handle each job.
  2. Separate required rules from preferences. Avoid double-booking first. Prefer suitable assignments where the plan still fits.
  3. Review the result. See who or what takes each job and which preferences the plan satisfied.
