Kubernetes for real-world work

Kubernetes finds where software workloads can run. Orchestrata finds where work fits when teams, equipment, and time are limited and rules have to be followed.

A plan your team can explain

See what changed, why it changed, and what to check before you change the plan.

What changed
When a specialist became unavailable, one interview kept its time with a qualified substitute. Another interview and the debrief that depended on it went to review.
Why
The second interview had only one offered time and no qualified specialist available at that time.
What to check
A coordinator checks for another qualified interviewer or offers a new time. The confirmed meetings stay in place.

Use your AI agent to prepare the work

AI can read messy instructions. Orchestrata checks which plans fit your rules without asking an AI model to judge each option. That means fewer decisions for the model to read and explain.

See the call-center results and how to reproduce them

In our call-center example, an exhaustive check evaluated 94,990 combinations across 60 independent staffing scenarios. It confirmed the best result for each one. That proof applies only to those scenarios and their rules. A plan that fits is not necessarily the best one. If a search ends early, the best plan found so far is only a partial result; if it finds none, the answer can still be unknown.
