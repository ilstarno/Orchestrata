Plan jobs, shifts, and deliveries

Orchestrata matches work to the people and equipment you have. Give it the jobs, the eligible choices, and the rules that must hold. It returns a plan you can review.

  • Assign technicians to jobs
  • Cover shifts after a callout
  • Match deliveries to vehicles

What you give it and what you get back

  1. List the work and eligible choices. Say which people, vehicles, or equipment can handle each job.
  2. Separate required rules from preferences. Avoid double-booking first. Prefer suitable assignments where the plan still fits.
  3. Review the result. See who or what takes each job and which preferences the plan satisfied.

Work through a planning example

Download sample work, check which rules apply, and compare the assignments with the expected result. The documentation shows how to run each example on your own machine.

Tell us what you need to plan

Describe the jobs, people or equipment involved, the rules that cannot change, and how you plan the work today. Use a fictional or anonymized example. Do not send customer records.
