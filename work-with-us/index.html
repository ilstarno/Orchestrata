Data safeguards

No local installation is needed for the hosted pilot. Before using real records, agree on hosting location, access, retention, deletion, and ownership.

  1. Scope one decisionTell us what you assign, who reviews the plan, what must never happen, and where planners can make a trade-off.
  2. Try one shift, one week, or one samplePick a representative slice and compare the proposed plan with how your team handles it today. Review exceptions with the people who make the plan.
  3. Agree on what to measureWith your team, choose a baseline and review criteria before testing. These could include unfilled assignments, rule violations, or time spent revising the plan.
  4. Plan the implementation with your teamIf the trial meets your criteria, discuss who owns the workflow, where it fits into your systems, and which enterprise modules would be relevant. Agree those details before implementation.
